Your first day, step by step.

Everything you need to start delivering with DoorStride in San Isidro, Gapan, Jaen, San Leonardo, Cabiao and San Antonio. Open daily, 6:00 AM – 11:00 PM.

Before day 1 Get set up

  • Go to doorstride.expo.app, tap Create an account and choose Rider. Enter your name, mobile number, email, motorcycle plate number and driver’s license number.
  • Upload clear photos of your driver’s license, a valid government ID, your motorcycle, and its OR/CR. Then wait for the admin to approve you; tap Check status to see if you’re in. If something needs fixing, the admin leaves a note and you can resubmit.
  • Add DoorStride to your home screen (Chrome: menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen; iPhone Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen) and open it from the icon.
  • Tap Turn on alerts, then Test sound. You should hear the new-order chime. Keep your phone off silent while you’re online.
  • Allow location and keep GPS on. Customers follow you on the map, and new orders go to the nearest rider.
  • In Account, add your GCash or Maya number so customers can pay you by e-wallet.

Getting orders Go online

On Jobs, switch on You’re online. When an order near you comes in, you hear the chime and get an offer. You have 30 seconds to accept; if you pass, it goes to the next rider. Orders nobody took show under Open orders, and you can take one from there.

You carry one delivery at a time. Food from a partner store comes to you once the store has accepted it, timed so you arrive when it’s ready.

The delivery From A to B

  1. 1
    AcceptThe order moves to Current delivery, with the pickup (A), any extra stops, and the drop-off (B).
  2. 2
    Navigate to the pickupTap Start navigation for turn-by-turn directions inside the app (spoken turns, the map follows you), or use Google Maps or Waze. Use Start navigation when you can: while Google Maps or Waze is on your screen, DoorStride can’t send your location, so the customer can’t see you moving.
  3. A
    At the pickup, tap I’m at the pickupFor Pabili, buy the items with your own cash; you get it back from the customer. If something is out of stock, follow the customer’s choice shown on the order (replace it, skip it, or call them first).
  4. 3
    Tap Picked upWith extra stops, go through each one first; the button says when you’ve picked up from all of them. Navigation switches to the drop-off by itself.
  5. B
    At the drop-off, tap I’m at the drop-offCan’t find the customer? Use Message customer or call the number on the order. If they shared their live location, directions go straight to them.
  6. 4
    Collect and completeCollect the exact amount shown (cash, GCash or Maya). Tap Complete delivery, take a photo of the delivered item (required), add the recipient’s signature if you can, then Mark delivered.

Camera not working? If the camera is blocked or won’t open, a Choose from gallery button appears so you can still add the photo.

Money What you earn

You keep 75% of every delivery fee. DoorStride’s share is 25%, rounded down to the whole peso: the centavos are yours. Tips are 100% yours. If the customer used a promo, DoorStride covers it; your earning doesn’t change.

Delivery fee: ₱60 for the first 2 km, then ₱15 per km, plus ₱15 for each extra stop.

Remit every day. At the end of each day, hand DoorStride’s share of your cash orders to the admin, in person or by GCash. The Earnings tab shows exactly how much. If you owe more than the cash limit, new orders pause until you remit.

Good to know Ride safe, ride smart

  • Use a phone mount, and only tap the screen when you’ve stopped. Spoken turns let you keep your eyes on the road.
  • Keep DoorStride open while delivering. Switching apps or locking the screen pauses directions and your live location.
  • Prefer Start navigation over Google Maps or Waze. It keeps sending your location the whole way, so the customer sees you coming and gets the “Your rider is almost there” alert. Use Google Maps or Waze only if you need traffic info.
  • A red “location isn’t being shared” message means your GPS is off or weak. Turn on high-accuracy location.
  • Be polite, wear your helmet, and handle food upright. Customers rate every delivery.
  • Go offline when you take a break, so offers go to riders who can take them.
